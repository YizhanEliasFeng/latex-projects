\section*{Abstract}

This report develops a simple ordinary differential equation model for antigen expression in an mRNA--LNP vaccine platform after intramuscular administration. The model links uptake, endosomal escape, mRNA degradation, translation, and protein clearance to antigen expression over time. Simulations show that, even at the same dose, changes in trafficking and stability parameters can produce either sharp transient expression or lower but longer-lasting expression. Overall, the model offers a biologically grounded framework for comparing vaccine designs and analyzing antigen kinetics.
